What is the genus summum? Being, an ambiguous word - in
one of its senses is the name for it. But we wish, not its name,
but its character.

To begin with then let us take all that we can. All that we
can take is what is in our minds & this as representation, mediate
or immediate. But we must abstract from all character of per-
sonality, since that is merely the limitation of our selection. The
character of all we can take is, mental representation; and the character
of all that can be taken is therefore representation, in general.

by us, are 1st in virtue of being taken by us mental feelings, and
