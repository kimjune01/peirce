2nd
Every character possessed by a number
is either such that any number whatever
is smaller than some number possessing
that character or else there is a number possessing that character
which is either larger than or identical with every number
possessing the character.

Or, every collection of numbers which has a number larger
than any of them has a number which is the largest among
them.

It is easy to conceive of a universe of numbers of which
this is not true. Thus if to the whole numbers we add
11, and 11·1, and 11·11 and 11·111, and 11·1111, and 11·11111,
etc without end, then although every single number would
have a number next greater than it, yet the class of numbers
smaller than 12 has no largest.
