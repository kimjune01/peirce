Triadic relations may be divided, upon the same principle as
dual relations, into, 1st, those which are constituted by {del}no relations{/del}
of similarity between the {del}obj{/del} three correlates, 2nd, those which are
constituted by dynamic relations between the correlates, and 3rd, those
which involve triple relations between the {del}formal{/del} correlates. Representations,
which are a sort of triple relations, may be divided in the same way.
