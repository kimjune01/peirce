Syllogisms of the 1:1 order.
Let the first premise be in Ă and β, the second
in C̆ and β̄. Then if in both premises β is connected
by addition, there will always be a single conclusion
which will be of the 2:d order. Thus
1 < Ă + p + β        1 < C̆ + p + β̄
1 < (Ă + p + β)(β̄ + p̆ + C) < Ă + p + p̆ + C

1 < Ă + p + β        1 < C̆(p + β̄)
1 < (Ă + p + β)(β̄ + p̆)C < (Ă + p + p̆)C
etc.
