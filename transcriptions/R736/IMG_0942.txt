The following are obvious complications
of the three forms of syllogism:


Complex Direct Syllogism.

If S, then M; If both M and N are true, then P;
Hence if both S and N are true, then P.


Complex major indirect Syllogism.

If both M and N are true, then P;
But from "If both S and N are true, Then P," {add}would{/add} follows Q;
Hence, from "If S then M" {add}would{/add} follows Q.
