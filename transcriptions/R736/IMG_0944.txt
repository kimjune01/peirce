The categorical syllogism is alone treated
in the common books. There is an elaborate
and rather trifling doctrine of its consisting
largely of terminology. It will be useful
to give a summary of it. {del}The three classes{/del}
The names of the three classes concerned in
the syllogism and represented by the three
areas in the diagrams are called the terms
of the syllogism. That which has the largest
area is called the major term; that which
has the smallest area is called the minor
term; the third is called the middle term.
The premises containing the major and
minor terms respectively, are called
the major and minor premises,
