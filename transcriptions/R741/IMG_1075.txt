MS 741

Logic

The more a symbol denotes, the more free
we are to apply it to what we please - the more,
therefore it approaches to being a mere sign -
and the greater becomes its relation to symbols
in general and as such - the more it increases
in extension.

The more qualities a symbol denotes - the more
perfectly it reproduces the manifold of the thing -
the more therefore it approaches a mere copy -
and the greater becomes its relations with the thing
- the more it increases in intension
