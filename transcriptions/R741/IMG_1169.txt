Lecture XIV.

General Method with the Logic of Relatives.
The premises are first multiplied together and
the operators Σ and Π are all brought to the
left of all the operands by the formulae

Πᵢ uᵢ . Πⱼ vⱼ = Πᵢ Πⱼ uᵢvⱼ.
Πᵢ uᵢ . Σⱼ vⱼ = Πᵢ Σⱼ uᵢvⱼ.
Σᵢ uᵢ . Σⱼ vⱼ = Σᵢ Σⱼ uᵢvⱼ.
To these formulae may be added the following:
Πᵢuᵢ + Πⱼvⱼ = ΠᵢΠⱼ (uᵢ + vⱼ).
Πᵢuᵢ + Σⱼvⱼ = ΠᵢΣⱼ (uᵢ + vⱼ).
Σᵢuᵢ + Σⱼvⱼ = ΣᵢΣⱼ (uᵢ + vⱼ).
