Relative addition and multiplication defined

Let the formulae
        (lb)ᵢⱼ = Σₓ lᵢₓ bₓⱼ
        (l†b)ᵢⱼ = Πₓ (lᵢₓ † bₓⱼ)

Multiplication a particular, addition a universal
mode of combination. The parts undistributed in
both. {del}Associative{/del} principles:
        l†(b†s) = (l†b)†s
        l(bs) = (lb)s.

{del}Two{/del} important formulae:
        l(b†s) -< lb†s
        (l†b)s -< l†bs,

{del}Formulae of transposition:{/del}
        (l -< b†s) = (b̆ -< s†l̆) = (s̆ -< l̆†b)
        (lb -< s) = (bs̆ -< l̆) = (s̆l -< b̆).

{del}Distributive principle:{/del}
        (l†b)s = ls + bs
        s(l†b) = sl + sb
        l,b†s = (l†s),(b†s)
        s†l,b = (s†l),(s†b).

Negatives of combinations:
        l†b = l̄, b̄        l,b = l̄ + b̄
        l̄†b̄ = lb          l̄b̄ = l†b.
