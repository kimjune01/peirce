derived from the distinction of Extension & Intension.

Intension is the reference of a representation to a quality,
Extension is the reference of a representation to an object.
In the representation the quality is immediately represented, and
if the representation is a concept the quality is thought. The object
however is mediately represented, that is it is thought of.
A proposition or judgement is the determination of one
representation or concept by a synthesis of it with another
