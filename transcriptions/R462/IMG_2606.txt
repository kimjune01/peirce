I should like to ask him whether there is no
such thing as a law of nature. I know very
well what his answer would be; namely that
a law of nature is a mere word. But to this
I should reply, it is {del}plainly{/del} undoubtedly of
the general nature of a symbol. But what
I asked was whether it has no being in nature
Because we can base predictions on it
which come true. He will reply they come
true {del}bef{/del} because nature is uniform. I reply
then events may be uniform by mere chance
without any special cause. The successive throws
of a die may be alike to any number. But nobody
bases any sound prediction on that, and that proves that
mere uniformity is not sufficient to support a prediction &
consequently that the laws of nature are not merely
uniformities among events.
