I define as follows:
        If there is no relation, ρ, such that every M is
        ρ to an N to which no other M is ρ, then, and
        then only, M > N

If M is not greater than N and N not greater than M
then M is equal to N.

From this point of view the difficulty is to prove
that it cannot be that M > N  N > M.  It is evident
that either a > b or b > a (or both) or a = b.

part is either {del}smaller{/del} {add}fewer{/add} than or equal to the whole
