Carried to Ecbatana as a slave, what would
such a man as Pythagoras inevitably do? He would make his
escape, without doubt. But look at the map of the surrounding
regions in an Atlas Antiquus, and remembering that Ecbatana
had only been captured by Cyrus two years before, and was then near
the eastern edge of the empire, say in what direction Pythagoras
would have made his escape. Surely not to the west; for so he
would have had to traverse the whole length of the Persian empire.
