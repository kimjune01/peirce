                                Copy.

                                                    January 12, 1903.

[hand: 2d copy]

My dear Mr. Gilman:

        The enclosed memorandum by Senator Lodge explains it-
self. I am very much interested in the case of Mr. Peirce. It
seems to me that he ought to be aided. Will you do whatever in
your judgment is wise in the matter?

                                Faithfully yours,

                                      THEODORE ROOSEVELT.


Dr. D. C. Gilman,
        President, Carnegie Institution,
             Washington, D. C.
