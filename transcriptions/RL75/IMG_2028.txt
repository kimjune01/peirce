No 30. On Systems of Doctrine.

Singularly enough, it seems to have been left to
me to make a first attempt to formulate in detail
what a system of doctrine ought to be. I follow the
same general theoretic method as in the memoir,
No 29, taking some of the most perfect systems ex-
tant, and imagining how they might be more rational.
