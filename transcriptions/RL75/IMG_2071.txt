I may say that I begin by dividing all signs into Icons,
Indices, and Symbols. An icon is a sign which is such
by virtue of a character which it might equally possess
if {add}the{/add} object it represents {del}did not exist{/del} {add}had no being{/add} (although of course
it could then not be a sign) and which it might equally
possess if it never were {del}regarded as a sign{/del} {add}interpreted in another sign.{/add} Thus
a chalk mark on a blackboard may serve as the Icon of
a geometrical line. This is because it is long and slender.
But it would be long and slender just the same even if
the geometrical line had no kind of being. An icon is
therefore a sign by virtue of its own Quality and is a
sign of whatever else partakes of that Quality. An index,
