                                               December 12,1902.


Mr. Charles S. Peirce,

        Milford,

              Pennsylvania.

Dear Mr. Peirce:-

        Immediately on receipt of your request for a copy of
your application I prepared a letter of transmittal and put with it a
typewritten copy from Dr. Gilman's file.  On looking further, however,
I could not find any other copy, apparently the original having been
referred to some Committee, so I have held it in order that at least
one copy should be within reach here.  I now write to tell you these
facts and to say that I hope to mail you a copy before very long.  I
may also add that I hope your case will have consideration even before
I have time to get that copy to you.

                                      Very truly yours,

                                                [hand: Marcus Baker]

                                                        Asst. Sec.
