                              Cambridge, Jan. 2, 1903.


Dear Mr. Gilman:
                              Professor James M. Peirce has asked me to support

the claims of his brother Charles for recognition by the Carnegie Insti-

tution.

               I do not know what Mr. Charles S. Peirce asks for, but I

suppose that the philosophers would put him forward as one of their best

exponents, and if Philosophy is to be recognized by the Carnegie Institu-

tion, I know of no philosopher who comes so near to having a raison d'être

as Charles S. Peirce.

                         * * * * * * * * *


                                             Cordially yours,

                                                  (signed)  John Trowbridge.
