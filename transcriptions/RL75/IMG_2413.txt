        List of Proposed Memoirs on Logic.

No I  On the Classification of the Theoretic Sciences of Research. This
will be a classification, not of possible sciences, but of the
sciences in their present condition; not of sciences in
the sense of "systematized knowledge," but of sciences in
the sense of living social studies of truth. The classifi-
cation is minute and covers the whole ground. For the
purpose of this memoir I propose to subject it to one
more severe criticism and revision. The outline of
the scheme is as follows (as near as I can carry it in my mind):

A  Theoretical Science,
        I. Science of Research.
             i Mathematics.
             ii Philosophy, or Cenoscopy.
                    1. The Doctrine of the Categories of Experience.
                    2. Normative Science.
