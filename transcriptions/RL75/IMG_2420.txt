[hand: No13.] On the Division of Logic. Logic is primarily divided
into Stechiologic, Critic, and Methodeutic, which are
defined in terms of the categories. Logic relates to Terms,
Propositions, and Arguments. Stechiologic treats of
every variety. Critic has no direct bearing upon
terms, upon Analytic, or Explicatory, Propositions, nor
upon Necessary Reasoning, as such. It does, however, treat
of meaningless and absurd terms, of {del}[unclear: false]{/del} {add}irrelevant{/add} definitions,
of fallacious demonstrations and of probable deductions.
