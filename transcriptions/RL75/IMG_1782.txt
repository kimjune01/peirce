of Induction and Hypothesis, and the conditions and grounds of their
validity in scientific investigation.  His mind is deeply occupied
with logical and metaphysical problems underlying the theory of Evo-
lution.  A series of articles which have appeared in recent years
in the Monist, a philosophical journal published at Chicago, give
some indication of the present tendencies and scope of his resear-
ches.  His case is one, therefore, which appeals to the student of
physical science, as well as to the metaphysician.
