D.C.G.4.

        I should suggest, as you seemed in our conversation to
think proper, that Mr. Peirce be offered whatever stipend is
deemed proper, in installments, payable on the completion and de-
livery of the manuscript of each one of a series of memoirs on
selected topics in Logic,- the list of topics to be approved in
advance by the authorities of the Institution.  With every hope,
my dear and honored friend and teacher, for your continued health
and prosperity, I remain,
                                Very truly yours,
                                        (Signed)  Josiah Royce.
