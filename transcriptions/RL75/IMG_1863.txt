Hon. H.H.D. PEIRCE,
                    Department of State, Washington, D.C.

Dear Sir:

          Will you permit me to say how important it seems to me is

the publication of your brother's, Mr. Charles S. Peirce's, papers in

Logic and philosophy, and to urge, through you, upon the Carnegie Com-

mittee that this is exactly the sort of work they desire to help.

                         Yours most sincerely,

                                      Percival Lowell.
