I stand responsible. I have often made this statement.
If it is not time, why am I not called upon to go ahead
with the printing?

Excepting in the case of one early paper on the Logic
of Mathematics, which I concluded I did not know
enough about to continue, I have never had a
disinclination to go on with any series of pub-
lications which I had begun. On the contrary,
the disinclination has always been on the side
of those who were to pay for the printing. When
such disinclination was manifested, of course I
ceased to press the matter.
