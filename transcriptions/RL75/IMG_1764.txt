I venture therefore to urge, most respectfully, but with the ardor of
a student of logic who much needs further light, and who hopes to get it from
Mr. Peirce,-- to urge, I say, that in due time his work be supported, if possi-
ble, in some way, by the Carnegie Institution, as a sort of work for which, as
I understand from you, the funds of the Institution can properly be used.

I should suggest, as you seemed in our conversation to think proper,
that Mr. Peirce be offered whatever stipend is deemed proper, in installments,
payable on the completion and delivery of the manuscript of each one of a series
of memoirs on selected topics in logic,-- the list of topics to be approved in
advance by the authorities of the Institution. With every hope, my dear and
honored friend and teacher, for your continued health and prosperity, I remain,

Very truly yours,

(signed) Josiah Royce.
