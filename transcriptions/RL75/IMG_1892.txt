            COLUMBIA UNIVERSITY
          IN THE CITY OF NEW YORK
              DEPARTMENT OF
      PSYCHOLOGY AND ANTHROPOLOGY

                 Garrison-on-Hudson,  N.Y.  January 25, 1901

                                                        [hand: check mark]


Dear Mr. Peirce:-

        It certainly seems to me that some of the income of the
Carnegie endowment might be used to advantage in enabling you to
complete your work in logic.    This will be a great contribution
to science.    I will write to Dr. Gilman as you request.   A
letter from me to Mr. Carnegie would, I fear, be of no advantage.
                                  Very truly yours,
                                      [hand: J McK. Cattell]



Mr. C. S. Peirce,
        Milford, Pa.
