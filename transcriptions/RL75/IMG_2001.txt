No.17.  On Terms.

This memoir will be based on my paper of November 1867.
Practice has shown that that {add}paper{/add} needs extension in several
directions. Besides, account has to be taken of important
classes of terms there barely mentioned. The historical
part, too, needs great amplification. My very conception
of what a term is has been much improved by studies
subsequent to that paper; and altogether original the study
of “agglutinative” languages has been an aid to me.
