                         Cambridge, Jan. 8, 1903.

Dear Mr. Gilman:

        Professor James H. Peirce has asked me to support

the claims of his brother Charles for recognition by the Carnegie Insti-

tution.

        I do not know what Mr. Charles S. Peirce calls for, but I

suppose that the philosophers would put him forward as one of their best

exponents, and if Philosophy is to be recognized by the Carnegie Institu-

tion, I know of no philosopher who comes so near to having a raison d'etre

as Charles S. Peirce.

                    * * * * * * * * *

                                      Cordially yours,

                                      (signed)  John Trowbridge.

[hand: Mr. Gilman [illegible] a strong opinion.]
