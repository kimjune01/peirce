Personal.

                    Clark University
                    Worcester, Mass.

President’s Office.

                                      January 3, 1903.

Hon. Herbert H.D. Peirce,
                    Washington, D.C.

My dear Sir,        I was for some time quite intimately asso-
ciated with your brother Charles, of whose abilities I have
an extremely high appreciation and to whose thought I am
greatly indebted for facts, ideas and stimulus.  It seems
to me that it would be a grave calamity to American thought
if his contemplated system should not be finished, and that
anything whatever that the Carnegie Committee can do, either
to accelerate or ensure this result, would be of the very
highest importance.
                    I am,
                         Most truly yours,

                              G. Stanley Hall.

[hand: Everybody knows Stanley Hall, President of the Clark
