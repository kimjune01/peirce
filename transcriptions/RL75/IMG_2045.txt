It is my belief that science is approaching a critical
point in which the influence of a truly scientific logic
will be exceptionally desirable. Science, as the outlook
seems to me, is coming to something not unlike the
age of puberty. Its old and purely materialistic concep-
tions will no longer suffice; while yet the great danger
involved in the admission of any other, ineluctable as
such admission is, is manifest enough. The influence
of the conceptions of methodeutic will at that moment
be decisive.
