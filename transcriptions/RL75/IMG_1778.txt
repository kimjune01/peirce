                              January 12, 1903.


My dear Mr. Gilman:

                    The enclosed memorandum by Senator Lodge explains itself.

I am very much interested in the case of Mr. Peirce.  It seems to me

that he ought to be aided.  Will you do whatever in your judgment is wise

in the matter ?

                              Faithfully yours,

                                      THEODORE ROOSEVELT.


Dr. D.C. Gilman,
        President, Carnegie Institution,
             Washington, D.C.
