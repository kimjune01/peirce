No.5.  On the Qualities of the Three Categories of Appearance. An analysis
and description of three irreducibly different kinds of elements found
in experience and even in the abstract world of pure mathematics. This
memoir rests upon Observation of the experience of every day and hour;
this observation being systematized by thought. It is proved, beyond
doubt, that there are no more than the three categories. The list was
first published by me in May, 1867; but has since been repeatedly sub-
jected to the severest criticism I could bring to bear upon it, with the
result of making it far more evidently correct. The categories were
originally called Quality, Relation, and Representation. The question of
names and other terminology for them still somewhat perplexes me.
I am inclined to call them Flavor, Reaction, and Mediation.
