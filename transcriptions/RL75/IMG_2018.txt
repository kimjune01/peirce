there are large deviations, even statistically. In the economics of
research the “laws” are mere general tendencies to which excep-
tions are frequent. The laws being so indefinite, at best, there is
little advantage in very accurate definitions of such terms as
“amount of knowledge”. It is, however, possible to attach a
