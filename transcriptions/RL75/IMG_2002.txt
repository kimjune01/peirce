23]        No 18.

On Propositions.

The question of the nature of the Judgment is today more actively
debated than any other. It is here that the German logicians are
best worthy of attention; and I propose to take occasion to give
here an account of modern German logic. Although this
seems rather the subject for a book than for a single paper,
yet I think, by sketching this memoir, I can bring into it
all that it is necessary to say about these treatises, which
belong to near a dozen distinct schools.

I shall then show how my own {del}doctrin{/del} theory follows
from attention to the three categories; and shall pass to an
elaborate analysis, classification, symbolization, [unclear: & doctrine]
of the relatives of propositions.

This will probably be the longest of all the memoirs, and
will balance No 16, which will be short. I think I shall
treat No 16 as a supplement to No 15 and divide No 21
into two parts to be handed in separately.
