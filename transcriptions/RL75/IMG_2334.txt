33. On Metaphysics. In this memoir I defend essentially Aristo-
telian opinions which give room for the real-being and agency
of ideas, distinguishing an esse in futuro from an esse in
praeterito and an esse in praesenti, and also a mode of
action substantially Aristotle's final causation as well as
physical action which is substantially his efficient causation.

34. On the Uniformity of Nature. There is a group of questions be-
longing to metaphysics, yet specially interesting to the logician
connected with the uniformity of nature. I propose in this memoir
to discuss all of these and to treat them historically.
