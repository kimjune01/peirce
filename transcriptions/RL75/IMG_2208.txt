                    But my studies were continued almost without
interruption. Whatever distractions from my solitary
position I might seek, a certain amount of work upon
my logic was a daily need. My perseverance was no
merit, any more than my perseverance in breathing.
The result has been that by this time I have built up
such an elaborate system, that the task of undertaking
to explain it is one of the utmost difficulty.
