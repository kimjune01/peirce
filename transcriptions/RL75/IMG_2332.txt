Let us start then with the theory of pure materialism. I say
the mind is nothing but the complex of a brain’s ideas, and
these ideas are mere aspects. That is an intelligible position.
There is no way of overthrowing it except by hard facts. I believe
that such facts abound. I propose to defend this proposition in this
memoir. I say, then, that if there were nothing but matter, there
could not be a law of nature. Well, there are no laws of nature,
