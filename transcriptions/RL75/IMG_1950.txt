No. 35.  On Metaphysics.
        The great distinction between Aristotelian philosophy and all
modern philosophy is that the former recognized a germinal mode
of being inferior to existence, which hardly Schelling does; certainly
no other modern philosopher.  This question is considered in the
light of the methodeutic developed in previous memoirs.  The result
is applied to all the questions of high metaphysics.
