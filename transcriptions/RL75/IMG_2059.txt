I have a reputation of not finishing things. I
suppose there is some basis of truth {add}beneath{/add} in it. But it has
been, like every evil reputation, exaggerated out
of all semblance to truth by calumny. It should
be remembered that I was connected for a long time
with the Coast Survey; and it will be easy for
members of the committee to ascertain that that
office has been, at times, a veritable hot-bed of
intrigue, and that I, in particular, have some
times suffered great injustice there. Voluminous
memoirs were prepared by me for publication
which I never could get printed; and then I was
accused vaguely and in intangible forms, of not
