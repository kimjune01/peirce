general maxim.

This consideration affects the method of least-squares, if this
method is looked upon in an extravagant theoretical manner; but not
if it is regarded as a way of formulating roughly an inductive inference.
Mr. Pearson's extensions, though they are excessively complicated,
and thereby violate the very idea of least squares, are not without value.
But other somewhat similar modifications of probability are
called for; and I shall endeavor to work out one or two
of them.
