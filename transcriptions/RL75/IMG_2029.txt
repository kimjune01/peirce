[hand: No 31.] On Classification.

        I study classification, after some general conside-
rations, by actually drawing up a number of classifi-
cations of the only sort of objects which we can suf-
ficiently comprehend; that is to say, different classes
of objects of human creation; such as, contrivances
for keeping the skin warm, languages, words, alpha-
bets, sciences, etc. From these, I endeavor to elicit a
general series of Categories of Classification.
