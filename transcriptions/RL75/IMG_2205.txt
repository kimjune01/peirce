To the Executive Committee of the Carnegie Institution

Gentlemen:
        I have the honor to apply for aid from the
Carnegie Institution in bringing into available form
the results of certain researches into logic. I will proceed
        1st, to explain what these researches are;
        2nd, to indicate how their importance may be estimated;
        3rd, to consider how far the aid of the Carnegie Institu-
tion is needed or desirable for the attainment of the purpose;
        4th, to suggest a plan by which sufficient aid
might be extended;
        5th, to estimate the probable ultimate total cost to the
Carnegie Institution of such aid so extended;
        6th, to inquire whether and how far this plan affords
a reasonable guarantee that the work will, in a
reasonable sense, be brought to completion.
