Passing now to the pure logical doctrine, an {del}proposition{/del} {add}assertion{/add}
is an act which represents that an icon represents the

a symbol. But its interpretant is an icon, a sort of compo-

seen. 'Mary' in like manner, is interpreted by a sort of
composite memory of all the occasions which forced
my attention upon that girl. The putting of these together
makes another index which has a force tending to
make the icon an index of Mary. This act of force
