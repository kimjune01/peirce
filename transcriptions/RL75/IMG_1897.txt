JOHNS HOPKINS UNIVERSITY,
BALTIMORE.

PRESIDENT'S OFFICE.

May 3, 1901.

Dear Sir:

        President Gilman wishes me
to say that he presented your request
to the Executive Committee at their
last meeting, and they referred it
to the Library Committee to consider
and report.  You may expect to hear
the conclusion before very long.

        [hand: Yours very truly]
        [hand: T. R. Ball]
        [hand: Registrar]

Dr. C. S. Peirce.
