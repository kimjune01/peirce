                         First Note

* My order of arrangement of the first eleven memoirs is subject to
reconsideration. The Categories are applicable to the logical analysis of Mathe-
matics. It is even a question whether this fact does not derange my classi-
fication, although I have carefully considered it, and have provisionally conclu-
ded that it does not. It further seems to me better to let the Categories first
emerge in the mathematical memoirs before explicitly considering them.
This is a question of methodic, which is not so exact in its conclusions as is
critical logic. I think the arrangement I here propose is favorable to the
reception of the Categories. But if I were to decide to postpone the mathe-
matical memoirs until after the categories, they might better be placed
last among the first eleven memoirs. In that case, also, and indeed
in any case, it might be well to place the memoir on the logical
conception of mind before {del}those{/del} upon Esthetics and Ethics.
The present arrangement has been pretty carefully considered;
and the last transposition is the only one that I think there is
much likelihood of my deciding upon. [hand: omit] After No 12, the only changes
possible in the list are shifts of boundaries in order to equalize the lengths
of memoirs.)
