War Department
Washington.



January 12, 1903.



Dear Lodge:

I have written to the Secretary of the Carnegie Insti-
tution that I want an opportunity at the next meeting of the Ex-
ecutive Committee to vote in favor of the proposed grant to
Charles S. Peirce.
                         Faithfully yours,
                                   Elihu Root.



Hon. H. C. Lodge,
        United States Senate.
