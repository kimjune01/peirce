To the Executive Committee of the Carnegie Institution

Gentlemen:

        I have the honor to apply for aid from the
Carnegie Institution in giving to the world certain researches
into logic, chiefly the logic of science. I will proceed
        1^st, to state what these researches are;
        2^nd, to show what their importance is;
        3^rd, to consider how far the aid of the Carnegie Institution
is needed, for the purpose;
        4^th, to suggest a plan by which the desired aid might
be afforded;
        5^th, to consider what the ultimate total cost is likely to
be;
        6^th, to ask what guarantee exists that the work will
in any sense, be brought to a conclusion.
