                         War Department
                          Washington.

                                      January 12, 1903.

Dear Lodge:

        I have written to the Secretary of the Carnegie Institu-
tion that I want an opportunity at the next meeting of the Executive
Committee to vote in favor of the proposed grant to Charles S. Peirce.

                         Faithfully yours,

                                      Elihu Root.


Hon. H.C. Lodge,

              United States Senate.
