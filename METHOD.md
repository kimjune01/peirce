# Editorial Method

How a Peirce manuscript becomes text here, and the discipline that governs it. Pipeline mechanics live in [`TRANSCRIPTION_PLAN.md`](TRANSCRIPTION_PLAN.md); what is already digitized at Harvard in [`hollis/digitized-crosscheck.tsv`](hollis/digitized-crosscheck.tsv); the state of other people's work in [`PRIOR_ART.md`](PRIOR_ART.md); draft-stage triage in `houghton-export/TRANSCRIPTION-TRIAGE.md`.

## Why

This continues Peirce's work, it does not archive it. He named the methodeutic and had no machine to run it. The point of transcribing him is to hand his inductive program the modern severe-testing machinery (e-values, anytime-valid inference, the line that runs from Peirce through Mayo to Vovk/Grünwald/Ramdas/Wang), to build a classification for evidence trajectories, and to put the result where anyone, a person or a coding agent, can use it. The transcription draws the provenance edge to the root of the canon: a text bound to its page image is replayable, so a reader inherits Peirce by checking the manuscript rather than trusting an editor. He never had a real critic in a university. The work he was owed is the constructive criticism and the improved version, given in homage, not extraction.

## Four layers, kept disjoint

The output is a typed ladder. Each layer is a different kind of entitlement, and they never leak into one another.

1. **Document.** What is on the page: deletions, insertions, and draught marks intact, gaps flagged rather than filled. Kill condition: the scan.
2. **Mind.** What he meant. Reconstructed intent (which draught supersedes, what a struck line was reaching for), citeable and defeasible.
3. **Critique.** Where he is genuinely wrong or short. A claim that can itself fail.
4. **Continuation.** The improved, extended version: the methodeutic operationalized, the math he reached for and did not have. Our claim on his lineage.

The rule that makes this honor him instead of overwrite him: the layers never leak. The instant a correction edits the transcription, or a reading of intent is passed off as what the page says, it repeats the attestation-for-verification failure the whole program exists to indict. Keep each layer independently replayable and a reader, or an agent, can climb from the improved version back down to the ink and check every step.

## The deletions are the point

A man with no critic was his own. A struck word is a killed claim, a rewrite is re-entry, six draughts are an inquiry trajectory: a hypothesis graph run by hand. The draught-to-draught diff is the abductor XOR, his cuts against his additions, the figure he changed against the ground he kept. Two consequences:

- The Document layer preserves the revision apparatus as primary evidence, not noise.
- The Critique layer only touches what survived his **own last revision**. Where he struck it, he already caught it; re-flagging it argues with a verdict he entered himself. The external critic is owed exactly the seam where his self-correction hit the wall of a method he did not have.

## Criticism is the main event

Charity is its setup, not deference. Reconstruct the strongest version of what he meant first, so the refutation lands on the real claim and not a strawman. Then mark the genuine errors plainly, with receipts: the evolutionary metaphysics (tychism, agapism) as untestable reach, the foundational holes in his frequentist probability, the flat technical mistakes any 2000-page corpus from a working mathematician carries.

Pretending everything he said was right is the real disservice, and it breaks his own rule: what cannot be false cannot be true, Peirce included. Make him unfalsifiable and you make him untrue. The charity bar only governs the *sort* (do not swing at gaps or prescience as if they were blunders), it never softens a real hit. Be slow to call it a mistake, because he is the canonical case of a thinker dismissed as wrong who was only early. Sort each finding into: wrong by his own lights (criticism), a gap later filled (continuation), or right but unread (vindication). Only the first is criticism, and shipping the third as a correction would dishonor the provenance.

## What is ours

A funded team is already doing raw VLM transcription and diplomatic markup of the *digitized* corpus, and doing it well (see [`PRIOR_ART.md`](PRIOR_ART.md)). Our distinct contribution, where effort goes:

- The **71 un-digitized items** outside their corpus (the cross-check proves the disjointness).
- The **Continuation layer**: the methodeutic operationalized, which no one else is building.
- The **representational reading of existential graphs as logic**, their named open problem and our home turf.
- An **agent-replayable, CC0** edition rather than a human-facing critical edition.
