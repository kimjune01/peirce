# Houghton Library Visit: Peirce Manuscripts

## Logistics

- **Library**: Houghton Library, Harvard University, Cambridge, MA
- **Collection**: Charles S. Peirce Papers, MS Am 1632
- **Access**: Open to all researchers. No affiliation required.
- **Procedure**: Request materials 2+ business days ahead via [HOLLIS](https://library.harvard.edu/how-to/access-materials-houghton-library). Appointment recommended.
- **Photography**: Personal phone photography allowed, no flash, no tripod.
- **Contact**: houghton_library@harvard.edu
- **Official page**: https://library.harvard.edu/libraries/houghton

## Priority 1: Must Photograph

These manuscripts directly underpin the methodeutics textbook.

| Robin # | Houghton call | Title / Description | Pages | Textbook ch |
|---------|---------------|---------------------|-------|-------------|
| R 682 | MS Am 1632 (682) | *An Essay toward Improving Our Reasoning in Security and in Uberty* (1913). The security-uberty tradeoff. Peirce's last major essay, written months before his death. | 53 + 10 variants | Ch 2 |
| R 683 | MS Am 1632 (683) | Security-uberty companion draft | ? | Ch 2 |
| R 684 | MS Am 1632 (684) | Security-uberty companion draft | ? | Ch 2 |
| R 637 | MS Am 1632 (637) | *Meaning Preface* materials (1909). Contains the passage: scientific retroductions are subject to considerations of economy. | ? | Ch 7 |
| R 638 | MS Am 1632 (638) | Continuation of Meaning Preface. Justification of retroduction, practical vs scientific retroduction. | ? | Ch 7 |
| R 606 | MS Am 1632 (606) | *Chapter III. The Nature of Logical Inquiry* (1905-06). Defines methodeutic as "the study of the proper way of arranging and conducting an inquiry." | 29 + variants | Index |
| R 1093 | MS Am 1632 (1093) | *Note on the Theory of the Economy of Research* (before June 1877). Original manuscript, 10pp. Published in Coast Survey report 1879. The first formalization of research allocation. | 10 | Ch 7 |
| R 690 | MS Am 1632 (690) | *On the Logic of Drawing History from Ancient Documents*. Mature treatment of abduction, evidence, economy. Published in CP 7.164-231 but variants matter. | ? | Ch 7, Ch 8 |
| RL 75 | MS L 75 | Carnegie Application (1902). Methodeutic as "theoretical study of advantages." The single most important methodeutic source on abduction-selection and economy. Large and complex document. | Large | Ch 2, Ch 7 |

## Priority 2: Worth Photographing

These fill out the methodeutic lecture sequence and late retroduction thinking.

| Robin # | Title / Description | Pages | Why |
|---------|---------------------|-------|-----|
| R 475 | Lowell Lecture VIII, *Abduction* | ? | More foundational for abduction than R 773 |
| R 476 | Lowell Lecture VIII (continuation) | ? | Same lecture |

| R 753 | *[Reasoning]* (~1906). Draft lecture on three kinds of reasoning. Identified by Bellucci/Pietarinen as draft on retroduction. | 5 + variant | Late retroduction |
